An interactive educational simulation lab built to make fine-tuning mechanics intuitive—visualizing how LoRA rank/alpha, gradient clipping, mixed precision, and learning-rate schedules affect convergence and stability.

## What You Can Explore

1. **LoRA Rank & Alpha**: See parameter count vs. adaptation capacity trade-offs
2. **Gradient Clipping & LR Schedulers**: Inspect loss spikes and warmup/cosine decay behavior
3. **Validation Splits**: Observe overfitting vs. generalization in real time
