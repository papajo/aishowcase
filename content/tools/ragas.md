Ragas helps teams quantify whether their Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline is actually retrieving the right chunks and answering without hallucination.

## Key Strengths

- **Reference-Free Metrics**: Measure faithfulness and answer relevance even without gold labels
- **Retrieval vs. Generation Split**: Pinpoint whether failures come from chunking/search or the LLM
- **CI-Friendly**: Run regression checks before shipping prompt or embedding changes

## Verdict

Essential for moving a RAG prototype from "looks okay" to measurable production quality.
