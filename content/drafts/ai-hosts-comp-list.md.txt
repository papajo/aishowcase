Critical caveats
1. Pricing pages change silently. All numbers here are from public docs as of mid-2026; verify before signing up to anything important.
2. "Fair use" clauses (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare) reserve the right to throttle or terminate aggressive usage even within free quotas — don't ship a viral app on free tiers assuming you'll quietly survive the spike.
3. Free-tier projects often lack business-grade features: backups (MongoDB Atlas M0), PITR (Supabase), advanced monitoring, custom auth flows, raw TCP, persistent containers (Render). If you need any of those, budget ≥$5/mo.
4. Two of your named providers are now off the free list entirely: Fly.io (no permanent free post-2024) and Glitch (hobby era ended). They appear in the report for completeness but their Avg reflects this.
